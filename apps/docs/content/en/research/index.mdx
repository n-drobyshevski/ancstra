# AI Research

Research in Ancstra is the work you do *before* a person, fact, or relationship lands in your family tree. It is a deliberate pipeline with three building blocks, designed so that nothing touches your tree until you choose to put it there.

```
Research items  →  Thread (investigation)  →  Factsheet (hypothesis)  →  Tree person
```

This section explains each building block in turn. Read [How Ancstra Works](/how-it-works) first if you want the bigger picture of the Discovery → Enrichment → Review → Promotion → Tree pipeline; the pages here zoom in on the *investigation* half of that flow.

## The building blocks

### Threads

A **research thread** is a chronological investigation journal — one open question, one running timeline of everything you and the AI assistant do to answer it. Items get attached, facts get extracted, factsheets get created and linked, and every action is recorded so you can retrace your reasoning later.

Read more in [Threads](/research/threads).

### Factsheets

A **factsheet** is a structured hypothesis about a person, a couple, or a whole family unit. It is the place where evidence accumulates before you commit to adding anyone to the tree. Each factsheet holds facts pulled from your research items, links to related factsheets, and a status that tells you whether it is ready to promote.

Read more in [Factsheets](/research/factsheets).

### Promoting to the tree

**Promotion** turns a factsheet into a real person on your family tree — either creating a new person from scratch or merging the factsheet's facts into someone who is already there. Linked factsheets can be promoted together as a cluster, in which case Ancstra wires the family relationships at the same time.

Read more in [Promoting to Tree](/research/promoting).

## Two kinds of promotion

The word "promotion" appears in two different places in Ancstra. They are related but distinct, and the docs treat them separately:

- **Item promotion** — converting a research item into a formal source with citations. Covered in [How It Works → Promoting to Sources](/how-it-works#promoting-to-sources).
- **Factsheet promotion** — converting a factsheet into a tree person (or a family cluster). Covered in [Promoting to Tree](/research/promoting).

<Callout type="info">
The AI assistant can suggest items to investigate, extract facts from documents, and propose relationships between existing persons — but it never modifies your tree directly. Threads and factsheets are the staging ground; promotion is the only doorway into the confirmed tree, and it is always your explicit action.
</Callout>
